# 🧑‍⚖️ AI Paralegal  
Your AI-powered legal assistant for drafting, summarising, and understanding legal documents.

AI Paralegal helps users generate legally accurate drafts, understand complex legal language, and follow step-by-step legal procedures — all without offering legal advice.  
Built for lawyers, startups, students, and anyone who needs quick legal clarity.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 📄 1. Smart Document Drafting  
Generate high-quality drafts such as:  
- NDAs  
- Rental Agreements  
- Partnership Deeds  
- Legal Notices  
- Employment Offer Letters  

Just enter inputs — the AI handles the formatting, tone, and structure.

---

### 🧠 2. Case & Document Summaries  
Upload long PDFs or paste text to get:  
- Bullet summaries  
- Key points  
- Timeline of events  
- Extracted actions & obligations  

---

### 📚 3. Legal Term Simplifier  
Explain legal jargon in **simple human language**.  
Example: “Indemnity clause” → plain English explanation.

---

### 🛣️ 4. Step-by-Step Legal Guides  
Get procedural roadmap for tasks like:  
- Filing a police complaint  
- Drafting a will  
- Starting a private limited company  
- Sending a legal notice  
- Registering a trademark  

---

### 🔐 5. No Legal Advice Disclaimer  
AI Paralegal **does not provide legal advice**.  
It only assists with drafting and understanding documents.

---

## 🏗️ Tech Stack (Example)
- **Next.js** (Frontend)  
- **Node.js / Express** (Backend)  
- **OpenAI API / LLMs**  
- **Supabase / Firebase** (Auth + DB)  
- **Stripe** (Subscriptions)  
- **PDF.js / LangChain** for document parsing
